      Event Horizon

      A flight school for gravity, time and black holes. Every mission is one idea from relativity, and you learn it by flying through it: why orbits are free fall, why GPS has to correct for Einstein, why the traveller comes home younger, what a horizon really is.

      Nothing here is faked with a fudge factor. The ship moves through the exact spacetime of a black hole, the clocks are integrated along your path, and the round window shows a ray-traced sky.
