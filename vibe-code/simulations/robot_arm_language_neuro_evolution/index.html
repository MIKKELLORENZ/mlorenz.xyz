Told What To Do

A five-joint robot arm, a table of coloured balls, and a sentence. “Pick up the green balls and put them in their respective bucket.” The sentence is turned into a vector by Qwen3-Embedding and fed to the arm alongside what its cameras see. Nothing is scripted, and there is no gradient descent anywhere — brains are mutated, scored and bred.
