Learning to Walk

A 65 kg humanoid with 18 servo joints and no idea what legs are for. It has to stand up out of a squat, hold its balance, then walk to waypoints — over slopes, up stairs, into the wind. Nothing is scripted and there is no gradient descent: brains are mutated, scored, and bred.
