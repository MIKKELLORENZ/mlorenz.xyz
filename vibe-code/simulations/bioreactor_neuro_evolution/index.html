Tending the Culture

A neural network runs a stirred bioreactor that turns stem cells into heart cells, neurons, β cells, kidney organoids or liver cells over two to four weeks. It sees only what a lab can measure, and every batch is also run by the published protocol on identical cells, so the two can be compared.
