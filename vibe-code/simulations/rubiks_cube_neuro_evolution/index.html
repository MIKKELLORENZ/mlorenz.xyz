Rubik's Cube Neuroevolution

A neural network that solves a Rubik's cube by looking at it. Its only input is a 32×16 pixel rendering of the cube from two corners — no facelet list, no colour counts, no solver. It answers with one of eight quarter turns, gets a fresh picture, and answers again.

No solver and no move search — just the picture and eight buttons. Two brains ship: the one that solves depth 7 was trained by gradient descent, and the genetic-algorithm champion this project is named for is one click away in the Brain panel.
