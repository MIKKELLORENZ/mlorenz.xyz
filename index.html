BioPilot

The operating system for cell therapy research.

Cell therapy labs run on a patchwork: an ELN for notes, a separate LIMS for inventory, FlowJo for FACS, spreadsheets for DOE. Nothing connects a protocol to its results. BioPilot is one platform with cell-therapy-native ontologies — built around how researchers actually work, from exploratory R&D through GMP.
